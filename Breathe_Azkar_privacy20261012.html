Privacy Policy for Breathe Azkar

Last updated: 2026-10-12

App: Breathe Azkar (تنفس الأذكار) · Package: com.nour.breathe_azkar · Developer: NOURELDIN

This Privacy Policy applies to the app Breathe Azkar (تنفس الأذكار) (package com.nour.breathe_azkar), developed and published on Google Play by NOURELDIN. We respect your privacy.

In short

Your adhkar, duas, Quran reading, collections, activity history and settings stay on your device. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we do not show ads or sell data. The app only sends data over the internet in the cases listed below: when you share your location for prayer times, when you download a background sound, when it checks for updates, and, only if you agree, when it sends anonymous statistics about usage and problems.

Anonymous usage and problem statistics (optional, off unless you agree)

When you first open the app, or the first time after updating, it explains that the app is developed and its performance improved through general statistics about how the app is used and the problems users run into, without touching any private information about your identity or your own use of the app, and asks whether you agree. Nothing is sent unless you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → General → Language → Privacy.

If you agree, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google) to send:

  • basic app and device information: the app version, the app language, the device model and Android version, and your country (Google works it out from your internet connection and does not store your IP address);
  • which features are used. For example, a session was completed (its section, and the name of the built-in collection, or simply "custom" for one you made), the Quran reader was opened in a given view, a routine was finished, a collection was created, imported or exported, or the settings were opened. It also sends the app language and colour theme you chose;
  • problems you run into, so we can fix them: when something goes wrong, for example the Quran pages fail to open, an imported file can't be read, or the app hits an unexpected error, a short note of what kind of problem it was and the technical name of the error. It never includes the error's message, its stack trace or any file names. Each problem is reported at most once each time you use the app;
  • a random identifier for this installation of the app, created by Google Analytics. It lets Google tell repeat use apart from new installs, and it is not linked to your name or account.

The statistics never include:

  • your name, email or any account;
  • your location or coordinates;
  • your device's advertising ID or Android ID (the app turns both off and does not request the advertising-ID permission);
  • any dhikr, dua or Quran text, the names or contents of collections you made, your reading position, or your activity history.

Advertising and ad personalisation are switched off, and the statistics are not shared for advertising. Statistics are kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. We use them only as totals, for example how many people use each feature, in which countries and languages, and on which devices. That tells us what to improve. If you turn statistics off after allowing them, the app also deletes the random installation identifier from your device. Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Google's Privacy Policy.

Location (prayer times and the Time Map)

To calculate accurate prayer times and your time zone, the app can use your device's location (Android permissions ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). The app asks for this permission the first time you open it. This is optional. You can decline the permission or pick your location on a map instead. Your location is stored in the app's local settings on your device.

If you decline and have not picked a place, the app does not show prayer times for a place you did not choose. Instead, the Time Map asks you to enable location to calculate prayer times. Tapping that note opens the prayer settings, where you can allow location or pick a place on the map. If you later allow location in your device's settings, the app uses it when you return to the app. If Android no longer shows the permission request, Use my location opens the app's page in your device's settings. Checking whether location is allowed happens on your device only, and the answer is never sent anywhere.

To provide this feature, the app sends the chosen coordinates to two public services. It does not send them to us, and never sends them to Google Analytics:

  • AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com), to fetch the prayer timetable for that place;
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim, to show the name of the place.

When you pick a location on the map, map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. These services receive the request, including your device's IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies. No name or identifier from the app is sent with it.

Background sounds

If you choose a background (ambience) sound, the app downloads that sound file from Google Drive. The download sends no personal information, though like any internet request your device's IP address is visible to the server.

Checking for app updates

When you open the app, it may fetch a small public file (hosted on GitHub) to check whether a newer version is available and what's new in it. This download does not include any personal information, account data or device identifiers, though like any internet request your device's IP address is visible to the server while the file downloads. We do not receive, log or store anything from this check. If you choose to update, Google Play's own in-app update feature handles the download and installation.

Permissions

Aside from the optional location permission described above, the app does not request sensitive permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage statistics described above are sent only if someone using the device agrees to them.

Your choices

You can turn anonymous statistics off at any time in the app's settings. Doing so stops them and deletes the installation identifier from your device. Because the statistics are anonymous and we hold no account for you, we cannot look up or single out any one person's data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this policy, and the app where needed, before the changes take effect.
