This Privacy Policy applies to the app Breathe Azkar (تنفس الأذكار) (package com.nour.breathe_azkar), developed and published on Google Play by NOURELDIN. We respect your privacy.

We do not collect data

We do not collect, use, store, share, or sell any personal information or device data from users of our app. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third‑party tracking SDKs.

Location (Time Map feature)

The app's Time Map feature can request access to your device's precise or approximate location (Android permissions ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) so it can calculate accurate prayer times and the correct time zone for your area. This is optional — you can decline the permission or set your location manually instead. Your location is processed entirely on your device, is stored only in the app's local settings, and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Permissions

Aside from the optional location permission described above, our app does not request sensitive permissions. If future versions request a permission to enable a feature, it will be optional, used only on your device to provide that feature, and not collected or transmitted by us.

Children

Our app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our practices change (for example, if we introduce analytics or accounts), we will update this policy and the app accordingly before any such changes take effect.
